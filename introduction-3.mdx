The **Refund** endpoint (`transactionType: "REFUND"`) is a server-to-server API used to initiate a full or partial refund for a successfully completed payment. It programmatically triggers a reversal of funds from your merchant balance back to the customer's original payment source (VPA/UPI, card, net banking, or wallet).

---

| **Environment** | **HTTP Method** | **API** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| `Sandbox` | `POST` | [https://api-uat.originpay.in/v2/order/command](https://api-uat.originpay.in/v2/order/command) |
| `Production` | `POST` | [https://api.originpay.in/v2/order/command](https://api.originpay.in/v2/order/command) |

## What This API Does

- **Automated Payment Reversal**: Initiates refunds directly from your backend without needing manual processing in the merchant dashboard.
- **Source-Account Credit**: Routes the refunded amount back to the customer's original payment instrument (UPI account, debit/credit card, or net banking).
- **Audit & Tracking**: Associates the refund request with the original `merchantOrderId` and generates a distinct refund reference for tracking and reconciliation.
- **Automated Settlement Adjustments**: Adjusts merchant ledger balances and logs refund events for accounting and compliance.

## When to Use This API

- **Order Cancellations**: Customer cancels an order before delivery or service provisioning.
- **Returns & Exchanges**: Product returned by customer according to your return policy.
- **Duplicate Charges**: Customer was billed twice due to network retries or accidental double payments.
- **Dispute Resolution**: Merchant proactively resolves a billing complaint or service dissatisfaction.
